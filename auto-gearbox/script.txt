How does an automatic car change gears with no clutch pedal? Let me show you inside.
First, the engine isn't bolted to the gears. It spins a fan inside a sealed case full of oil. The oil hits a second fan and turns it. That's why you can stop in Drive without stalling. At cruising speed, a clutch locks the two fans together, so less power is wasted.
Next, the clever part: planetary gears. A sun in the middle, planets around it, and a ring outside.
Hold the ring still, and the planets crawl around slowly. That's low gear, for maximum pull.
Lock everything together, and it spins as one. Direct drive.
Hold the planet carrier, and the ring spins backwards. Reverse!
Clutches pushed by oil grab and release these parts in a split second, and a computer decides when.
Modern ten-speeds stack four of these gearsets.
