This whole video was made with code. No editing app. It's called HyperFrames.
HyperFrames turns a web page into a video. You write HTML, and it renders every frame into an MP4.
So anything a web page can do, your video can do.
Kinetic text. Smooth animation. Real 3D. Charts that count up. Captions synced to your voice. Transitions, sound effects, even a whole website, turned into a video.
And the best part? You don't write the code. Claude does.
