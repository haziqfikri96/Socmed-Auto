Part 2: your first real HyperFrames video 🌋

1. Start a project:
npx hyperframes init my-video
cd my-video

2. In Claude Code, describe it: "Using /hyperframes, make a 20-second vertical video about how volcanoes erupt. Bold text, dark theme."
Be specific: say vertical and how long.

3. Preview and edit on the timeline:
npx hyperframes preview

4. Render: draft while you tweak, high quality for the final:
npx hyperframes render --quality draft
npx hyperframes render --quality high
