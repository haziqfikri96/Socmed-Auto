What would happen if you jumped into a hole going straight through the Earth?
At first, you'd just fall. But gravity keeps pulling, so you'd get faster and faster.
By the time you reach the centre, you'd be falling at more than twenty-eight thousand kilometres an hour.
Right at the centre, gravity pulls equally from every side, so you'd feel weightless. But you're going way too fast to stop.
You'd shoot up the other side, slow down, and pop out on the far side of the planet, about thirty-eight minutes after you jumped.
Then you'd fall right back in.
Luckily, this can't actually happen. The core is about as hot as the surface of the Sun.
