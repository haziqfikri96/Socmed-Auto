Does swallowed gum really stay in your stomach for 7 years? 🫧 Nope! Your stomach strips off the sugar and flavor, but the rubbery gum base can't be digested, so your gut's muscle waves just push it along and out in a few days. (Swallowing LOTS of gum at once can cause a blockage, so spit it out when you're done 😅)
