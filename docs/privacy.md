# Privacy Policy — Haziq AI Auto Poster

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Haziq AI Auto Poster ("the app") is a personal tool that uploads videos to the TikTok account of the person who authorises it. This page explains what information the app handles.

## Information the app receives

When you connect your TikTok account, TikTok provides the app with:

- your basic profile information (an account identifier, display name and avatar)
- access and refresh tokens that allow the app to upload videos to your account

The app does not receive your password, your messages, your contacts or your followers' details.

## How the information is used

The information is used only to upload videos to the connected account and to confirm which account is connected. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

## Storage and sharing

- Tokens are stored privately in the developer's own environment and are not published.
- The information is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. Videos and their captions are sent to TikTok in order to upload them.

## Retention and removal

Tokens are kept only while the account stays connected. You can end the connection at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security & permissions → Apps and services, after which the tokens stop working. You can also ask for stored information to be deleted using the contact below.
